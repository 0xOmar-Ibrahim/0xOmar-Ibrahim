## 👤 About

I'm **Omar Ibrahim**, an Information Technology student and Junior Penetration Tester based in **Cairo, Egypt**.

My work centers on how applications, networks, and systems behave under adversarial conditions. I find weaknesses through **manual testing**, **vulnerability research**, and **systematic attack-surface analysis**, and I validate findings myself rather than relying solely on automated scanners.
